Our story

A local smart home business for normal homeowners.

Snake River Smart Homes was created to help homeowners in Idaho Falls, Rexburg, and nearby Southeast Idaho communities make their homes safer, smarter, and easier to manage without feeling overwhelmed by technology.

Owner Nicson White focuses on practical systems that people can actually understand and use. That means listening first, explaining options clearly, installing devices cleanly, and making sure every customer feels comfortable before the project is complete.
